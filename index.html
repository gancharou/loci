Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Loci is a personal map app for saving places that matter to you. We built it with one principle in mind: your data belongs to you. Loci has no accounts, shows no ads, and never sells or shares your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Loci — maps, markers, names, descriptions, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it, and it is never sent to us.

What we collect

To keep the app working and improve it, Loci uses a few services provided by Google. Here is exactly what they receive.

Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). We record how the app is used: for example, that a map was created or a marker was added, how many markers a map has, whether a photo was attached, which map type or theme is selected. Analytics never include the names, descriptions, coordinates, or photos of your markers. Firebase also receives an app-instance identifier and, like any internet service, your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location (country or city). Loci does not collect your advertising ID.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, we receive a technical report: the error details, device model, Android version, app state, and an installation identifier. Reports do not contain your name, email, or the content of your maps.

Maps and search (Google Maps Platform).

  • Map tiles are loaded from Google for the area you are viewing.
  • When you search for a place, your search text and the visible map area are sent to Google Places to return results.
  • Map previews on the home screen are generated by Google Static Maps from the coordinates of the markers on that map.
  • If you grant location permission, your location is used on your device to center the map and show your position.

Why we collect it

Only to make the app work (maps, search, previews), to understand which features are used, and to find and fix crashes. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with anyone other than the service providers listed above, which process it on our behalf.

Security

All data sent from the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are stored by Google Firebase according to its retention settings. Because Loci has no accounts, this data is not linked to your name or email, and we cannot identify you from it. Your local data is deleted when you delete a map or uninstall the app.

Backups

Android may automatically back up app data, including your maps and photos, to your own Google account. This backup is controlled by you in your device settings; we have no access to it.

Export and sharing

You can export your maps as ZIP files and share them with anyone you choose. This is entirely under your control — we are not involved in this process.

Children's privacy

Loci is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
