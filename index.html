Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Loci is a personal map app for saving places that matter to you. We built it with one principle in mind: your data belongs to you.

What we collect

Nothing. Loci does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your maps, markers, photos, and notes stay on your device.

Data storage

Everything you create in Loci - maps, markers, descriptions, and photos - is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data.

Third-party services

Loci uses Google Maps to display map tiles. Google's own privacy policy applies to map data. We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash reports, which helps us fix bugs. No personally identifiable information is included in these reports.

Export and sharing

You can export your maps as ZIP files and share them with anyone you choose. This is entirely under your control - we are not involved in this process.

Children's privacy

Loci does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
